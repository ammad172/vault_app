# VaultLock Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** December 18, 2025

## Introduction

VaultLock ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our password manager application.

## Data Collection and Storage

### What We Collect
- **Local Data Only**: All your vault entries (passwords, usernames, notes, etc.) are stored EXCLUSIVELY on your local device in an encrypted format
- **No Analytics**: We do not collect any usage analytics or telemetry data
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifying information

### Where Your Data is Stored
- **Local Device**: Your encrypted vault database is stored locally using AES-256 encryption
- **Optional Cloud Backup**: If you choose to enable Google Drive backup, your encrypted vault data is stored in your personal Google Drive account's app-specific folder
  - This data is encrypted before upload
  - Only you have access to your Google Drive
  - We cannot access your Google Drive data

## Third-Party Services

### Google Sign-In (Optional)
- Used only if you choose to enable cloud backup
- Subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Allows access to your Google Drive for backup purposes only
- You can revoke access at any time through your Google Account settings

### Apple Sign-In (Optional, iOS/macOS only)
- Used only if you choose to enable Apple Sign-In for authentication
- Subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- Minimal data sharing

### In-App Purchases
- Processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store
- Subject to their respective privacy policies
- We do not collect or store payment information

## Data Security

### Encryption
- **Master Password**: Hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations
- **Vault Data**: Encrypted using AES-256 cipher
- **Encryption Key**: Stored securely in your device's keychain/keystore

### Local Security Features
- Biometric authentication (fingerprint/Face ID) where supported
- Auto-lock after configurable timeout
- Rate limiting on unlock attempts (maximum 5 attempts per 30 seconds)

## Your Rights

### Data Control
- **Full Ownership**: You own all your data
- **Export**: You can export your vault data at any time
- **Delete**: You can delete the app and all associated data from your device at any time
- **No Account Required**: VaultLock works entirely offline if you choose not to use cloud backup

### Data Retention
- We do not retain any of your data on our servers
- All data is stored on your device or your chosen cloud provider (Google Drive)
- Deleting the app removes all local data

## Children's Privacy

VaultLock is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
